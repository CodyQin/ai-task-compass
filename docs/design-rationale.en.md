# Design rationale: entrepreneurial learning and AI engineering

[Overview](../README.md) · [中文](design-rationale.zh-CN.md)

Sources motivate choices, not certify this toolkit. Distinguish research/practice, our adaptation and untested effectiveness.

| Choice | Primary reference | Adaptation and boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Discover before prescribing | [Steve Blank: Customer Development](https://steveblank.com/category/customer-development-manifesto/) | Record assumptions, users/buyers and external learning tests. Synthetic personas are not market evidence. |
| Bound exposure before forecasting | [Effectuation: affordable loss](https://effectuation.org/the-five-principles-of-effectuation) | Agree time/cash limits and stops. This does not establish profitability. |
| Separate fixed and adaptive control | [Building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents) | Use quadrants within solution design; simpler routes first. Our matrix is not the article's taxonomy. |
| Define success before selection | [Demystifying evals for AI agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents) | Use output contracts, representative/exception cases and error rubrics; no small-sample reliability claims. |

Changes: problem-first entry, stage selection, one shared brief, falsifiable learning experiments, exposure/stop conditions and permission to skip irrelevant stages.

Check usefulness on authorized, anonymized tasks: repeated questions, retained facts/corrections, actionable next steps, acceptance clarity and review/rework cost. Evidence may show a module is too heavy; simplify it. Stars and extensive documentation are not proof of user or business value. Urgent one-step tasks may need no full planning process.

Primary references checked 2026-11-07. No papers redistributed, no endorsement implied, no controlled business-outcome study claimed.
