# Pilot and value review · 试点与业务价值复盘

Respond in the user's language. Reuse the problem, solution and evaluation briefs. Before a pilot, agree scope, acceptance owner, authorized samples, proposed quality thresholds, human approvals, time/cash exposure, review date and stop conditions. Do not require a large rollout to learn whether one step is useful.

Distinguish hypotheses, conditional estimates and measured results. Compare the same workload, period and quality target. Track preparation/execution/review/rework, maintenance and setup separately; avoid double-counting. Released hours are capacity, not automatically reduced payroll or revenue. Financial benefit needs substantiated expense reductions, incremental margin or avoided loss, with overlapping benefits removed.

List upfront cash, ongoing model/tool/maintenance cash and attributable human resource estimates separately. No payback date with nonpositive net cash or zero/missing upfront basis. If quality has not met the business gate, label financial estimates provisional rather than declaring success. Use ranges/sensitivity only with explicitly supplied assumptions, not invented probabilities.

For actual trials, record what changed, evidence, errors, quality/time/cost and whether the key assumption survived. Recommend continue, revise, stop or gather specific missing evidence. Identify owner, rollback/intervention and next review. Content pilots must also consider audience/factual/editorial quality; more outputs or views are not automatically incremental profit.

Output a pilot card and decision log, not a financial guarantee. Protect confidential material. This skill plans and reviews; it does not deploy, contact users, buy tools, publish, approve business actions or make payments.
