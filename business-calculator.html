先算清时间，再讨论收益
Time and cash are different

统一按“每月”记录，所有金额使用同一货币。结果是假设下的静态测算，非收益承诺。
Monthly basis, one consistent currency. Conditional, static estimates—not promised returns.

时间 / Capacity

初始搭建时间另列在工作表，未在月度释放工时中扣除。负值表示增加工作量。
Record one-time setup hours separately. Negative released time means more work.

现金 / Cash

填写条件后计算。Enter assumptions, then calculate.
公式与边界 / Formulas and limits

释放工时 = 次数 ×（原总分钟 − 新执行分钟 − 新检查返工分钟）÷ 60 − 新增维护工时。
Released hours = runs × (old total minutes − new execution − new review/rework) / 60 − extra maintenance hours.

月净现金 = 现金节省 + 增量毛利 + 减少损失 − 新增费用 − 维护现金。
Monthly net cash = savings + incremental margin + avoided loss − incremental fees − maintenance cash.

投入为正且月净现金为正，才显示静态回本月数 = 投入 ÷ 月净现金。首年 ROI =（12 × 月净现金 − 初始投入）÷ 初始投入。投入为零时不除以零；未过质量/收益确认条件时不展示 ROI。
Payback requires positive outlay and net cash. First-year ROI = (12 × monthly net cash − outlay) / outlay. No division by zero; quality/benefit gates apply.

未自动计入增长、折现、税、爬坡或未列费用；12 个月保持同一假设。人工资源价值不自动变成现金收益。可能相关或重叠的收益不能重复相加。
No automatic growth, discounting, tax or ramp assumptions. Staff capacity is not automatically cash benefit. Do not add overlapping benefit categories.

纯本地计算，无模型调用、无分析脚本、不上传或保存输入。服务托管方有其访问记录。
Local calculation only; no model calls, analytics, task upload or persistence. Hosting has its own access logs.
